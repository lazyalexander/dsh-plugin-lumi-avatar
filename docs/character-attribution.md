# Character attribution and distribution status

Lumi Companion for DeepSeek is an independent community project, not an official DeepSeek character or product. The current release is a public preview that includes the model and ten adapted motion clips; it is not a code-only package.

## Illustration

Original character: 上善无形「溟月」. Maid whale-girl derivative: ZipZipPipe. The illustration comes from a public open character gallery: [gallery](https://iphsiibl.github.io/deepseek-whale-girl-gallery/) · [per-image attribution](https://github.com/Iphsiibl/deepseek-whale-girl-gallery/blob/main/ATTRIBUTION.md).

Illustration-derived work follows [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/): attribution, noncommercial use, indication of changes, and ShareAlike for adaptations. Lumi modifications include 3D construction, facial proportions, hair and clothing, rigging, expressions and runtime adaptation.

## Head

The head is a substantial rebuild on an open-source base model: skull proportions, facial features, hair, rigging and expressions were redone for Lumi, so the result differs significantly from the base.

Original credit: Sour暄 / 幻象黑兔, Sour式空_夏卉 FA361. The [original ReadMe](../renderer/licenses/Sour-original-ReadMe.txt) is bundled unchanged; this summary does not modify its terms.

## Motions

The ten clips are part of this project's own motion set. The shipped player keeps upper-body and finger tracks, with local standing support controlling feet and pelvis, so playback is a standing interaction rather than a full-body recording. See [motion design](motion-sources.md). No third-party motion captures, Gokuraku Jodo VMD or music are distributed.
